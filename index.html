About Primax Prevarsity

Founded in Ibadan, Oyo State, Primax Prevarsity is a prevarsity institution empowering secondary school leavers to conquer university entrance exams. Located at Sango, Bembo, Poly Road, Ibadan, we simplify complex syllabi, especially Physics and Chemistry, to make learning accessible and enjoyable.

Mission: prepare students for the future through rigorous exam training (UTME/JAMB, JUPEB, WAEC, NECO), personalised counselling and holistic development. Vision: build resilient youths who discover their purpose and excel in life.
